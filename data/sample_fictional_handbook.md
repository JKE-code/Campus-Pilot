## §1.0 Campus Code & Academic Timings
### §1.1 Academic Hours and Attendance Policy
1. Academic instruction runs Monday through Friday from 08:30 AM to 04:30 PM.
2. Minimum attendance requirement for appearing in end-semester examinations is 75% in each registered course. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% due to certified medical reasons must apply to the Academic Dean for condonation. Students below 65% attendance are detained.
3. For academic queries, timetable clashes, or attendance discrepancies, students must contact the **Academic Affairs Office** at `academic-affairs@sample-college.test` or visit Ground Floor, Administration Block.

### §1.2 Central Library Regulations
1. The Central Library is open from 08:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, and 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM on Saturdays and Sundays.
2. During examination weeks, the library reading room remains open 24/7.
3. Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for a loan period of 14 days. A late return fine of ₹5 per day per book applies.

## §2.0 Hostel Regulations, Night Curfew & Outpass Policy
### §2.1 Hostel Curfew and Attendance Check
1. The campus main gate and all hostel blocks enforce a mandatory student curfew at **09:30 PM** daily.
2. Evening biometric roll call is conducted in each hostel common room between 09:30 PM and 10:00 PM.
3. Unauthorized presence outside hostel premises after 09:30 PM without a verified Outpass is considered a category-A disciplinary violation.

### §2.2 Digital Outpass & Gate Clearance Rules
1. Any resident student seeking to leave campus overnight, during weekends, or beyond curfew hours must submit a digital **Hostel Outpass Request** via CampusPilot.
2. **Automated Guardian Voice Verification:** Upon submission, an automated voice call is dispatched to the student's staff-verified parent/guardian contact number. The guardian must verbally confirm permission.
3. **Mandatory Warden Authority:** Following successful parent verification, the request advances to `awaiting_warden_approval`. Final gate clearance authority rests strictly with human hostel wardens. AI and automated agents **never auto-approve** outpasses.
4. **Security Gate Validation:** Approved outpasses generate a cryptographically signed QR gate pass valid for 48 hours. Security gate personnel scan the pass at Gate 1 upon exit and return.
5. For emergency outpass queries, contact the **Hostel Warden Desk** at `warden@sample-college.test`.

## §3.0 Facilities, Maintenance SLA & IT Services
### §3.1 Facilities Maintenance and SLA Turnaround
1. All facility defects (plumbing, electrical fixtures, washroom amenities, civil repairs) must be reported through CampusPilot.
2. **Standard Resolution SLAs:**
   - **Emergency / High Urgency (water supply failure, electrical hazard, elevator stoppage):** 4 hours maximum response time.
   - **Medium Urgency (lighting failure, fan malfunction, tap leakage):** 24 hours turnaround.
   - **Low Urgency (furniture repair, paint touch-up, notice board fixes):** 72 hours turnaround.
3. Facilities coordinators can be reached at `facilities@sample-college.test`.

### §3.2 Campus Wi-Fi & Computing Infrastructure
1. The campus high-speed Wi-Fi network (`Oakwood-Secure`) is accessible across hostels and academic blocks using standard student roll number credentials.
2. Personal routers, unauthorized VPN gateways, and packet sniffing tools are strictly prohibited.
3. For network connectivity outages or password resets, contact the **IT Services Helpdesk** at `it-services@sample-college.test` or Room 104, Technology Tower.

## §4.0 Disciplinary Committee, Grievance Redressal & Anti-Ragging
### §4.1 Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Regulation
1. Oakwood Institute enforces an absolute zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in any form, direct or indirect, physical or verbal.
2. Any proven act of ragging results in immediate suspension from academic programs, cancellation of hostel allotment, and filing of a First Information Report (FIR) with civil law enforcement.
3. The 24/7 Anti-Ragging Vigilance Committee is headed by the Proctorial Board and can be contacted at `antiragging@sample-college.test`.

### §4.2 Student Grievance Redressal Mechanism
1. General student grievances concerning grading transparency, faculty conduct, or campus amenities can be formally submitted through CampusPilot as confidential grievances.
2. Grievances are reviewed by the Student Welfare Committee within 7 working days.

## §5.0 Emergency Services, Health Center & Campus Security
### §5.1 Campus Health Center & Medical Emergencies
1. The Campus Health Center operates 24/7 with a resident medical officer and basic emergency life-support ambulance stationed behind Hostel Block C.
2. Consultations and basic medications are provided free of cost to registered students.
3. In case of acute medical emergencies, contact the Health Center emergency coordinator at `health-center@sample-college.test`.

### §5.2 Campus Security Patrol
1. Campus Security personnel are deployed across all entry gates, perimeter pathways, and academic quadrangles 24 hours a day.
2. Visitors must present government-issued photo identification and log entry details at Gate 1.
3. The Security Control Room can be reached at `security@sample-college.test`.
